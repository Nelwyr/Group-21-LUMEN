The trade-off

Scoring, sources and limits

Each criterion is scaled across these five cities: 100 × (city value − lowest value) / (highest − lowest). The index is the sum of the three scaled values × your normalised weights. A criterion identical across all cities gets 50 for everyone. Zero means lowest in this comparison, not no opportunity.

Wellness density is a proxy: the share of Urban Wellness Professionals among all respondents in each city. This is survey composition, not a measured population share or a count of wellness venues. The survey has not been population-weighted. City purchase intent is context only.

Reliability is a sample-size flag, not statistical confidence: n ≥ 15 reliable; n = 8–14 directional; n < 8 insufficient. Only city-level inputs enter the ranking. All segment-level averages are diagnostic only; intent is withheld for n < 8. Ranking is unavailable if a candidate city's total sample falls below 15.

The regional shares and growth rates are illustrative case assumptions. This screen does not estimate LUMEN market share, city acquisition costs, distribution access or city ROI. Small score gaps do not establish a meaningful difference.
