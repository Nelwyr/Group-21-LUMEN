How to read these results

Clients acquired = budget / CAC. Acceptance is a separate survey estimate; it is not applied a second time to customer acquisition. Increasing budget scales customers, not per-customer returns.

LTV and payback assume stable monthly purchases and an extrapolated customer lifetime calibrated from the historical LTV treated as net revenue. These are scenario assumptions, not observed German retention.

The leadership assessment uses €2.19 as a premium-price proxy for Jonas and the 3:1 return target for Elena. It does not measure brand perception or prove a launch recommendation. Under a fixed lifetime, LTV:CAC and payback are mathematically linked.

Awareness, prices and interpretation

Awareness = respondents recognising a brand / all respondents in the selected segment, across cities. Counts come from the case's synthetic customer survey; percentages are displayed rounded to whole numbers. The four segments are never pooled into a German average. Awareness is not perceived quality, preference or willingness to pay.

Prices come from competitor_prices_by_channel.csv, using single 330ml cans only. Multipacks and subscriptions are excluded. Changing channel moves prices, while awareness continues to describe the selected segment as a whole. Changing segment preserves both axis scales. The price axis expands if LUMEN exceeds the displayed range.

LUMEN has no measured awareness in this survey. Its full-height vertical line encodes only the simulator's retail price; it has no dot or awareness value. The nearest-price assessment uses listed brands in the chosen channel; equal distances use the numbered brand order. This comparison does not adjust the simulator's acceptance or ROI.

Each brand card gives the recognition count and segment sample size. Samples of 8–14 are directional; awareness below n = 8 is withheld. Sample sizes are not confidence intervals or evidence of population representativeness. The browser loads prepared prices and segment aggregates only.

The trade-off

Scoring, sources and limits

Each criterion is scaled across these five cities: 100 × (city value − lowest value) / (highest − lowest). The index is the sum of the three scaled values × your normalised weights. A criterion identical across all cities gets 50 for everyone. Zero means lowest in this comparison, not no opportunity.

Wellness density is a proxy: the share of Urban Wellness Professionals among all respondents in each city. This is survey composition, not a measured population share or a count of wellness venues. The survey has not been population-weighted. City purchase intent is context only.

Reliability is a sample-size flag, not statistical confidence: n ≥ 15 reliable; n = 8–14 directional; n < 8 insufficient. Only city-level inputs enter the ranking. All segment-level averages are diagnostic only; intent is withheld for n < 8. Ranking is unavailable if a candidate city's total sample falls below 15.

The regional shares and growth rates are illustrative case assumptions. This screen does not estimate LUMEN market share, city acquisition costs, distribution access or city ROI. Small score gaps do not establish a meaningful difference.
