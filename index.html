Awareness, prices and interpretation

Awareness is the share of respondents recognising each brand within the selected segment of the synthetic customer survey. It is not perceived quality, preference or willingness to pay. LUMEN awareness is unmeasured; its full-height line indicates only the selected price.

Prices use comparable single 330ml cans from the case, excluding multipacks and subscriptions. Missing listings are not plotted. The nearest-price assessment uses listed brands in the selected channel; ties use brand order. Cards show recognition counts and sample sizes; n = 8–14 is directional and n < 8 is withheld.

Changing segment or comparison channel updates this chart only. The price comparison above keeps the recommended 45/40/15 channel mix and the engine’s overall acceptance curve; it does not estimate segment-specific acceptance.
